Hi all, this is the first and probably last publically released version of Quiver Fortess.

It's been a little bit under a year since I announced the cancellation of the mod, and so I felt like it was a good idea to release a representative version of the mod to the public.

What was Quiver Fortress, you may ask?

It was a work-in-progress TF2 mod that wanted to feel like TF2 2007. But it also wanted to bring back cut items. But it also got inspiration from past TF games. But it also wanted to restore the older balance for weapons. But it also wanted fast-paced movement and armor.

As you can tell, this mod had a bit of an identity crisis, which was problematic when trying to give the mod an identity. It's all over the place.

Near the end of development, bunny hopping and most custom weapons were removed as I wanted to rebuild the mod from the ground up. However, this last-ditch effort didn't revive the mod and it was later cancelled.

This public version of the mod has been uploaded here for historical reasons. The source code remains on https://github.com/BitlDevelopmentStudios/source-sdk-2013-bds-base if you wish to look at how the mod functioned, and maybe integrate some of its features into your TF2 mod. This version of the mod is under the quiver_PUB branch.

this PUB build readds all the cut weapons removed during the attempt to revive the mod. the cut weapons were removed from the items_custom, but were easy enough to readd. The Afterburner no longer has its original custom model and killicon as they were leaked community made content, and I didn't feel comfortable distributing the leaked content in this public version.

Things have been polished up slightly and basic testing was done to ensure everything worked. If you have feedback for the mod, don't provide it. The mod is dead.

I hope you enjoy this beautiful mess of a mod I made. If you want to see a more focused mod, check out ANTICITIZEN ONE. https://www.moddb.com/mods/anticitizen-one-bitl
